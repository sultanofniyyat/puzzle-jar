Sweet Stacks is the color sort game in Puzzle Jar and the second stop on the level road. Glass jars each hold up to four sweets in mixed colors; you move sweets from jar to jar until every jar holds four of one color and gets a lid. It plays like a ball sort or water sort puzzle, with no clock and no move limit, and here Undo is always free.

How do you play Sweet Stacks?

Tap a jar to lift the sweet on top, then tap the jar you want it in. A sweet can only land on a sweet of its own color or in an empty jar. When four sweets of one color sit together in a jar, the jar gets a lid and is finished. Put a lid on every jar and the level is won.

Why does Sweet Stacks move more than one sweet at a time?

Lifting a jar takes the top sweet together with every sweet of the same color directly under it, because a run of one color always travels as a group. They then fill the jar you tap only as far as it has room: a jar holds four, so if it already has three, only one sweet goes across and the rest stay where they were. Look at how much space a jar has before you choose it.

Why won't a sweet go into the jar I tapped?

That jar is already full with four sweets, it already has its lid, or its top sweet is a different color. The game tells you which one it was in a short message. A sweet never goes on top of another color, even for a moment, so the only places it can go are a matching top sweet or a completely empty jar.

What are the wrapped sweets in Sweet Stacks?

Sweets in a purple wrapper with a question mark, which hide their color until they become the top sweet of their jar. They appear from level 31 on hard and boss levels. Because you cannot see what is underneath, these levels reward clearing the jars that hold wrapped sweets early, so you find out the colors while you still have space to use what you learn.

What is the best strategy for Sweet Stacks?

Keep an empty jar empty for as long as you can. An empty jar is the only place any color can go, so filling it with a single sweet just to make some move throws away your most flexible space. Prefer moves that put a sweet on its own color, and before each move, ask which jar it frees up. Work toward a lid: a finished jar takes four sweets off the board for good.

Which jar should I work on first in Sweet Stacks?

Usually the one closest to being done or closest to being empty. A jar with three of a color on top needs only one more to get its lid; a jar with just one or two sweets in it can often be emptied in a move or two, which gives you a fresh empty jar. Jars that are full of mixed colors are the slow ones, so leave them until you have room to unpick them.

Is undo free in Sweet Stacks?

Yes, always, and there is no limit. Sweet Stacks is the one game in Puzzle Jar where Undo never costs anything, on every level, with or without Remove Ads, and Again (start the level over) is free too. In the other games only the first three take backs of a level are free, unless you own Remove Ads. So in Sweet Stacks you can try an idea, see where it leads and step back as many times as you need.

What happens when there are no moves left in Sweet Stacks?

A "No moves left!" card appears with your choices: add one empty jar, undo a few moves to try another order, or start again. The level waits for your choice; nothing runs out while you think. Sometimes you are not truly stuck but going round in circles between two empty jars; that is why the +1 Jar button sits in the bottom bar the whole time, not only on that card.

What does the +1 Jar button do?

It adds one empty jar to the level, which is often all a hard sort needs. You can use it once per level. The first one can come from a short video you choose to watch; otherwise it uses one rescue from the count that all 20 games share. Once used on a level, the button shows a zero until the next level.

What does a hint show in Sweet Stacks?

A hint lifts the right jar for you and puts a gold ring on the jar to drop the sweets in, so you only have to tap the ringed jar. The move comes from the same solver that checks our levels, which looks for the shortest sensible way to finish, so a hint is a move a good player would make. Each hint uses one from your shared hint count.

How many colors are there in Sweet Stacks?

Up to 10. Level 1 has only three colors, then a new one comes in about every six rounds of the road. Each color also has its own small symbol, a heart, shield, star, tick, bolt, eye, triangle, plus, music note or gear, so two similar shades are never a guessing game. The symbol shows on the sweet and again on the lid of a finished jar.

How hard does Sweet Stacks get?

Gentle at first: level 1 cannot be lost and levels 1 to 5 are only easy or medium. After that it follows the road's rounds of easy, medium and hard, with a boss on every fourth hard level. More colors and the wrapped sweets are what make later levels hard. Every level is played through by our solver with no extra jar before it ships, so the extra jar is a shortcut, never a requirement.

Is Sweet Stacks like water sort and ball sort games?

Yes, it is the same family of color sort puzzle, played with sweets in jars instead of liquid in test tubes or balls in tubes. If you enjoy those, the rules will feel familiar within a level. What we changed is around the edges: free unlimited undo, a symbol on every color, wrapped sweets for later levels, and ads only between levels, never inside one.
