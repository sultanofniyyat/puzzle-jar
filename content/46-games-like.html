Is Puzzle Jar connected to any of these games?

No. Puzzle Jar is made by a small independent studio, and we have no link with any studio or game named on this page. Our games are our own designs in well-known genres, with their own pieces, rules and levels; a nut sort or a bus jam is a genre, the way "crossword" is. We name other games here only so you can find something you will enjoy, and we stick to plain, checkable facts about them.

What games are like Screw Jam?

Screw Jam, from Rollic Games, is one of the best-known screw puzzles, free on iPhone and Android. Fans of the genre like the slow, satisfying clear-out and the moment a whole layer drops away. Our Bolts & Jars plays in the same family: plates held by screws, jars that take three screws each, and a 5-hole tray for spares. Ours has no clock or move limit, and every screw color carries its own symbol. Many apps share the "screw puzzle" name, so check the developer before you install.

What games are like Screw Jam without ads?

Honestly, a free screw puzzle with no ads at all is hard to find, because most are paid for by ads. In Puzzle Jar the ads come between levels, never during one, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase takes away the full-screen ones in all 20 games, including Bolts & Jars. If you want a phone with no ads anywhere, Apple Arcade is the cleaner route: Apple states its games come "without ads or in-app purchases" (Apple), though it is a paid subscription and its puzzle line-up changes.

What games are like Nut Sort?

"Nut Sort" is used by many apps; the biggest include "Nut Sort: Color Sorting Game" by Tripledot Studios. People like the clink of nuts stacking and how a messy board turns tidy. Our game is also called Nut Sort, after the genre; it mixes bolt heights (tall ones hold 5, helper bolts 2 or 3) and, on harder levels, dark nuts whose color shows only when they reach the top. If you want the same idea without metal parts, Water Sort Puzzle by IEC Global is the classic liquid version.

What games are like Water Sort Puzzle or Ball Sort?

Water Sort Puzzle (IEC Global) and Ball Sort Puzzle (HM Games) are the two famous names of the color sort genre: tidy containers of mixed colors into single-color ones. Sweet Stacks is our version with sweets in jars; undo and restart are always free in it, and harder levels add wrapped sweets that hide their color. On Apple Arcade there is "Water Sort Puzzle: Get Color+" from Tripledot Studios, for subscribers.

What games are like Royal Match?

Royal Match by Dream Games is a match-3: swap neighboring pieces to make lines, clear goals within a move count, and restore a castle between levels. Bead Swap is our match-3, with needles, bursts and rainbow beads, wax and crates to clear and brass buttons to bring down. It has no castle or story, and the beads that drop in are fixed for each level. For another big match-3 with a story, Candy Crush Saga by King is the long-running alternative.

What games are like Royal Match without in-app purchases?

Look at subscription services rather than free games, because most free match-3 games sell boosters. Apple says its Arcade games have no in-app purchases, and its puzzle list changes, so browse it for a match game you like. Puzzle Jar does sell things (hint packs, take backs, rescues and Remove Ads), so it is not the answer to this exact search; what we can say is that every Bead Swap level is proved winnable with no help, and there are no lives to buy back. Full details are on our free and ads page.

What games are like Candy Crush?

Candy Crush Saga, made by King, is the best-known swap-and-match game on phones, free to download with purchases on iPhone and Android. In Puzzle Jar, Bead Swap is the closest, played with beads on trays from 7x7 to 9x9. If you like the candy world but want something other than swapping, Fruit Tumble clears groups by tapping. Outside our app, Royal Match and Toon Blast are other well-known names in the same genre.

What games are like Candy Crush with unlimited lives?

Puzzle Jar has no lives at all, so you can retry a level straight away as often as you like. That applies to Bead Swap, our match-3, and to every other game in the app. Be clear about the trade: levels still have a move limit (15 to 38 moves in Bead Swap), so you can lose a level; you just never wait to try again. Before installing any other match-3, search its store description for "lives" or "energy" to see how it handles retries.

What games are like Block Blast?

Block Blast!, from Hungry Studio, is the well-known fit-the-pieces-and-clear-lines game. Mosaic Tray uses the same line-clearing on an 8x8 tray, but it is built in levels: you collect jewels with a limited set of pieces dealt in a fixed order, so a level never fails on a bad random hand. If you want endless high-score play, stay with Block Blast; Tetris by PlayStudios is the other famous line-clearer.

What games are like Block Blast with no ads?

The simplest answer is Block Blast's own Apple Arcade edition: "Block Blast!+" from Hungry Studio is listed on Apple Arcade, and Apple says Arcade games have no ads or in-app purchases. On Android there is no Arcade, so most free block games carry ads. In Puzzle Jar, a single Remove Ads purchase stops the full-screen ads in Mosaic Tray and in our other 19 games together; optional reward videos stay, but only play if you choose them.

What games are like Tile Busters or Triple Tile?

Tile Busters (Spyke Games) and Triple Tile (Tripledot Studios) are triple tile games: pick uncovered tiles, collect three alike on a bar, and clear the pile. Our Button Tin uses sewing buttons in up to 5 layers and a 7-space card; later hard levels add face-down buttons you must uncover. Tile Explorer by Oakever Games is another popular choice. On their Google Play pages, checked on the US store on 2026-11-24, Tile Busters did not carry the "Contains ads" label, while the others did.

What games are like Zen Match?

Zen Match, by Moon Active, sells itself on relaxation: triple tile matching with a calm look. If the calm part matters most to you, choose games that do not rush you. Button Tin has no clock and no move limit; a level ends only when the card is full. If you also want no ads at all, an Apple Arcade subscription is the route Apple describes as free of ads and in-app purchases.

What games are like Goods Sort?

Goods Sort, by Shinrays Games, has you sort small products on shop shelves into groups of three. Shelf Sort is our goods sort: 12 kinds of goods, each with its own outline shape, and locked shelves that open by playing, never by paying or watching an ad. There is no timer and no move count; after 40 moves without a pop, a friendly card asks if you want help.

What games are like Hexa Sort?

Hexa Sort, published by Lion Studios Plus, is the best-known hexagon stacking game: drop stacks of colored tiles on a honeycomb and watch matching tops hop across and merge. Honey Hive is ours, themed on honey: three stacks are offered at a time, 10 of a color on top are collected toward the level goal, and later levels add iced stacks and locked cells. The stacks you are dealt are fixed for each level rather than random.

What games are like Bus Jam?

Bus Jam, from Rollic Games, is the crowd-to-bus sorting game: send passengers to the bus of their color without clogging the waiting spots. Bus Out is another well-known one. Hop On is our bus jam. It always shows the buses coming next, uses a bench of 4 to 7 seats, and adds hidden and frozen passengers, doorways and big buses as you go. It has no clock, so you can plan every tap.

What games are like Unblock Me or Parking Jam?

Unblock Me (Kiragames) is the classic slide-the-blocks-to-free-the-red-one puzzle, and Parking Jam 3D (Popcore) has you drive cars out of a packed lot. Fans like clear logic and the "aha" when one move frees everything. We have two in that spirit. Toy Chest slides colored blocks out through doors of their color. Pencil Case is a tap-away puzzle where pencils leave tip first. Kiragames also sells a paid Premium edition of Unblock Me.

What games are like Knit Out or Yarn Fever?

Knit Out (Rollic Games) and Yarn Fever (Brave HK) are recent yarn-themed puzzle games. Snug Skeins is ours: felt toys lose a layer of wool with each tap, baskets collect each color, and a small sewing tin holds spare wool. A tap that would overfill the tin is simply refused rather than losing you the level.

What games are like Twisted Tangle?

Twisted Tangle and Tangle Master 3D, both from Rollic Games, are rope puzzles: move the ends until the knot comes apart. Garland Tangle is our rope untangle, with strings of lights between posts; a string lights up once it crosses nothing, and steel lanterns that cannot move appear later. For a free, ad-free classic, Simon Tatham's Puzzles includes a game called Untangle (Simon Tatham).

What games are like Suika, the watermelon game?

Suika Game, by Aladdin X, is the drop-and-merge game where fruit combines up to a watermelon. On phones it is a paid app, and Apple Arcade also lists "Suika Game+". Treat Drop is our merge drop game with 10 sizes of sweet, from a sprinkle up to a rainbow lolly. The difference: Suika is about the highest score in one long game, while Treat Drop gives you levels with a goal, such as "make 2 tarts", and a set supply of sweets.

What games are like Bubble Witch 3 Saga?

Bubble Witch 3 Saga (King) and the "Bubble Shooter" apps from Ilyon are the best-known bubble shooters on phones. Fans like aiming bank shots and dropping big clusters at once. Bubble Picnic is ours: a dotted guide shows every bounce and the landing spot, you can swap your next two bubbles for free, and the ceiling never comes down on you. Levels ask you to clear the field or free stars with a set number of bubbles.

What games are like LinkedIn Queens?

LinkedIn's Queens is a daily logic grid: one queen in every row, column and colored region (LinkedIn help). Players often compare it with Star Battle logic puzzles. Quilt Pins is our Star Battle-type game: one pin per patch, row and column, pins never touching, on quilts up to 11x11, and a hint that explains its reasoning. Unlike a once-a-day puzzle, it has a ladder of thousands of quilts. LinkedIn's other daily games, such as Tango and Zip, are in the same place on LinkedIn.

What games are like Toon Blast?

Toon Blast, by Peak, is a tap-to-blast game: tap groups of matching cubes to clear them and make rockets and bombs. We have three blast games, each with a twist. Fruit Tumble is the classic falling kind. In Balloon Bunch balloons float upward. Tide Pool pushes a new row up every few taps, so you race the tide by moves, not by a clock.

What games are like Flow Free?

We do not have one, to be honest. Flow Free (Big Duck Games) is a connect-the-colors path puzzle, and Puzzle Jar has no game where you draw lines between dots. If you have Apple Arcade, "Flow Free+" is the same game without ads. Our nearest in feeling is Garland Tangle, which is also about lines that must not cross, though you move lanterns onto posts rather than draw paths. The free Simon Tatham's Puzzles collection has several path and loop puzzles as well.

What games are like Two Dots?

Two Dots, by Playdots, has you draw lines through dots of the same color to clear them. Puzzle Jar has no line-drawing game, so there is no direct match. If what you enjoy is clearing groups of one color to meet level goals, Fruit Tumble and Balloon Bunch give that feeling with a single tap per group. If it is the drawing itself, look at Flow Free instead.

What games are like 2048?

2048, the web game by Gabriele Cirulli, merges numbered tiles; Threes! (Sirvo) is a paid sliding-number game with an Apple Arcade edition. Puzzle Jar has no number games, so we are not a fit if numbers are what you like. If it is the merging you enjoy, Treat Drop joins two equal sweets into a bigger one, with no numbers, but with physics and aim instead of sliding a grid.

What games are like Tetris?

The official Tetris app on phones is published by PlayStudios. Puzzle Jar has no falling-block game and no speed pressure, so it is a different experience. The nearest is Mosaic Tray: you place pieces on a grid and clear rows and columns, but at your own pace, with no clock. For something closer to Tetris itself, stay with the official app.

What games are like Unpacking or A Little to the Left?

Unpacking (by Witch Beam) and A Little to the Left (Secret Mode) are cozy tidying games: unpack a home, or straighten and sort everyday objects. Unpacking is a paid app, with an Unpacking+ edition on Apple Arcade; A Little to the Left is free to try with a one-time full-game unlock. Puzzle Jar is not a story game like these, but tidying is its heart: Shelf Sort arranges goods on shelves and Sweet Stacks puts sweets in order.

What games are like pin pull puzzles?

Pin pull games, where you slide out pins to let sand, water or treasure fall the right way, are a separate genre, and Puzzle Jar does not have one. Many people searching "screw pin puzzle" mean screw games instead; if so, Bolts & Jars fits. Be careful with pin-pull ads in general: the UK's Advertising Standards Authority ruled in 2020 that some pin-pull ads for decorating games were misleading because the games were mostly something else (ASA).
