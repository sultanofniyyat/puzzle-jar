Treat Drop is the drop and merge game in Puzzle Jar, the kind where fruit or sweets fall into a jar and join. Slide to aim the sweet held over the glass jar and let go to drop it. When two of the same sweet touch, they join into the next size up. Each level asks you to make certain sweets, such as two tarts, from a set number of drops, while keeping the jar below the dotted line.

How do you play Treat Drop?

Touch the screen and slide left or right; the sweet above the jar follows your finger. Let go and it drops, rolls and settles against the others with a small bounce. Two of the same kind that touch join into the next kind. The goals at the top count what you have made, and the pill on the right shows how many sweets you have left to drop.

What are all the sweets in Treat Drop, from smallest to biggest?

There are ten, each made by joining two of the one before: sprinkle, gumball, mint, macaron, cookie, donut, tart, pie, cake and rainbow lolly. Two rainbow lollies that touch burst and leave the jar. Only the two to five smallest kinds are ever dropped; everything bigger you have to build.

What happens if sweets go over the dotted line?

If a sweet comes to rest above the dotted line near the top of the jar, or stays above it for about 2 seconds, the jar has overflowed and you are stuck. A sweet that is still falling through the line does not count. While anything is above it, the line pulses red as a warning.

How do you win a level of Treat Drop?

Make every sweet the goals ask for before your drops run out. Goals go up to pies: making cakes would take far too many drops for one level. On some levels the goal is two kinds at once, as in the picture, where one pie and five donuts are wanted.

How do you lose in Treat Drop?

Two ways: you run out of sweets before the goals are met, or the jar overflows past the dotted line. In practice, running out of sweets is the more common one. Either way the game offers a rescue, a take back, a new try or the way home.

Can you see which sweet is coming next?

Yes, one ahead. The next sweet waits in the small NEXT box on the right side of the jar. The order of drops is fixed for each level, so a level always gives the same sweets in the same order, and a second try lets you plan with what you learned.

Does it matter where I drop each sweet?

Yes, a lot. Sweets grip each other and tend to stay close to where they land, so dropping everything in one spot rarely builds what you need. We widened the jars and tuned the friction so that dropping in one spot wins far fewer hard levels than aiming does. Aim each sweet at its twin, or at a gap where its twin will land next.

What is the best strategy for Treat Drop?

Keep the big sweets together on one side and the small ones on the other. A pile sorted by size lets each new join land next to the one it should join next, which can set off a chain. Look at the NEXT sweet before you drop the current one, and leave room for it. Try not to bury a small sweet under big ones, where its twin cannot reach it.

Why are there sweets already in the jar at the start?

From Treat Drop level 10, some levels begin with a few sweets placed in the jar. They can be a head start, if they match what you need, or an obstacle you have to build around. Either way, the level was won by our computer player with exactly that start.

What does the Treat Drop hint do?

It shows the best spot for the sweet you are holding. Before answering, it tries every spot with the jar's real physics and plays the next four sweets ahead. In our tests it played about as well as the best tenth of our computer test players, where a hint that only looked at the matching sweet was merely average.

What does the Treat Drop rescue give you?

More sweets to drop, from 3 to 18 depending on the level. If you got stuck because the jar overflowed, the rescue also lifts the little sweets out of the jar so there is room again. A level allows up to two rescues.

How is Treat Drop different from endless merge games?

It has levels with goals and a fixed number of sweets, instead of one endless jar played for a high score. It also starts gently: level 1 asks for one cookie and shows the best spot for every sweet, and cannot be lost. Ads appear only between levels, never after drops.
