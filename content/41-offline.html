Yes, Puzzle Jar plays fully offline. All 20 games, every level and the daily puzzles are stored inside the app, so they work in airplane mode, underground or anywhere with no signal. Only ads, purchases, Restore purchases and the privacy questions need internet, and your progress saves on the phone either way.

Can you play Puzzle Jar without wifi?

Yes. Puzzle Jar needs no wifi and no mobile data to play. The levels are built into the app when you download it, so nothing has to come from the internet when you start a level. You can open the app with the phone in airplane mode and play the level road, Free play or today's puzzles exactly as you would at home.

Do all 20 games work offline?

Yes, every one of them, from Bolts & Jars to Route Duel. None of the 20 games has an online-only mode, a live event or a level that streams in. Route Duel's rival is played by the phone itself, not by another person over the internet, so even the duel works on a plane.

What in Puzzle Jar needs an internet connection?

Four things, none of them needed to play:

  • Ads: full-screen ads between levels and the rewarded videos you choose.
  • Buying anything, and the prices in your currency.
  • Restore purchases, which asks Apple or Google what you own.
  • Google's privacy form in Europe and the UK, and changing your Privacy choices.

The Starter Pack card is also only shown when the store can be reached.

Does Puzzle Jar use mobile data?

Mostly for ads. A level itself needs no data, but if mobile data is on, the app downloads ads in the background and fetches store prices, and on Android it also sends small Google Analytics notes, such as a won level. We have not measured how many megabytes that adds up to, so we won't quote a number. Video ads are usually the biggest part, so if your plan is tight, the next answer shows how to switch data off for the app alone.

How do I stop Puzzle Jar using mobile data?

Turn mobile data off for this one app. On iPhone: Settings, then Mobile Data (Cellular in the US), scroll to Puzzle Jar and switch it off. On Android, open Settings, find Puzzle Jar under Apps, then its mobile data or data usage entry, and turn off mobile data use; the exact names differ by phone maker. The game keeps working on mobile; ads and purchases wait for wifi.

Can I play Puzzle Jar in airplane mode?

Yes. Airplane mode only cuts the connection, and the game does not need one. Your levels, the jar on Home, your helper counts and your day streak all keep working. With the connection off, most full-screen ads cannot load, and you won't be able to buy anything or watch a rewarded video until you are back online.

Is Puzzle Jar a good game for a long flight?

It suits one well: one download gives you 20 games that never ask for wifi. You can stop at any moment for landing or a meal cart, since there is no clock in 19 of the 20 games, and a level left halfway simply starts again later. Bring headphones or turn the sound off in Settings. If you want a wider list for travel, see our best puzzle games guide.

Can I play Puzzle Jar underground or with a patchy signal?

Yes, and a weak signal does not freeze the board. The levels never wait on the network. The only things that try to use the connection are ads and the store, and if no ad has finished loading when a level ends, you go on without one, sometimes after a short pause (never more than about 15 seconds) while the app tries to reach the ad network. Rewarded video buttons may say the ad isn't ready yet; try again once you have signal.

Are the daily puzzles available offline?

Yes. "Today's puzzles" are already inside the app, one for each game, picked by the date on your phone from puzzles we shipped with it. Nothing is downloaded each morning, so a new set is ready after midnight even with no signal. Solving them offline still fills your jar and keeps your day streak going.

Does my progress save when I play offline?

Yes. Progress is always written to the phone itself, online or not, the moment a level ends: your road level, each game's own level, the jar, the day streak and your helpers. There is no server copy to sync later, so you lose nothing by playing for a week without a connection.

Do rewarded videos work offline?

No. A rewarded video has to download from Google's ad network before it can play, so with no internet the "watch ad" buttons cannot give a free hint, take back, rescue or extra Solo Run time. The game tells you the ad isn't ready rather than pretending. Hints, take backs and rescues you already have still work as normal.

Can I use hints offline?

Yes, any hints, take backs and rescues you have saved work with no connection, and the 3 free take backs on each level do too. What you can't do offline is top up: buying a pack and watching a video for +1 both need internet. One top-up does work offline: every full jar on Home still gives +1 hint, +1 take back and +1 rescue.

Does Remove Ads still work when I'm offline?

Yes. Once you own Remove Ads, the game remembers it on the phone, so it never has to check with Apple or Google before each level. Your unlimited take backs keep working offline too. Only on a new phone or after reinstalling do you need a connection once, to tap Restore purchases in Settings.

Can I buy hints or restore purchases offline?

No. Every purchase and every Restore goes through the App Store or Google Play, which need a connection. Offline, Restore purchases answers "Couldn't reach the App Store" or "Couldn't reach Google Play", and no money is taken. Wait until you have signal and try again; nothing is lost by waiting.

What happens when I go back online?

Very little, and that's on purpose. Your progress is already on the phone, so there is nothing to upload or merge. Ads start loading again, so a full-screen ad can follow the next level once one has arrived, and the shop shows prices in your currency again. If a purchase was interrupted by losing signal, the store usually finishes it the next time the app opens online.

Should I download Puzzle Jar before a trip?

Yes, on wifi, before you leave. The iPhone download is {{IOS_SIZE}}, so it is better done at home than on airport wifi. Then open the app once while you are still online and answer its first-launch questions, play a level, and you're set. If you want Remove Ads or a hint pack for the trip, buy it before you go, because the shop needs a connection.

What happens if I open Puzzle Jar for the first time with no internet?

It still starts. On the first launch the app asks its privacy questions: Apple's tracking question on iPhone, plus Google's consent form in Europe and the UK. Google's form needs a connection, so offline it is skipped and asked again later. The game never waits longer than 15 seconds for those questions before letting you play, and every level works from the start.

Does Puzzle Jar drain the battery?

We haven't measured battery use, so we can't give hours or percentages. What we can tell you: the game draws at most 60 frames a second, and it never needs the network to run a level. For a long day away from a charger, lower the screen brightness and use airplane mode, which also stops ads from downloading in the background.

Does the day streak work offline?

Yes. The streak counts each day in a row on which you finish a level in any game, using the phone's own date, so a week of offline play keeps it alive. The one part that needs internet is saving a broken streak: that uses a rewarded video, which can't play without a connection. You can do it that day or the next once you have signal.

Does Solo Run work offline?

Yes. Solo Run, the timed mode inside Route Duel, has its boards stored in the app like every other level, and its clock runs on the phone. The only part you lose offline is the once-a-run rewarded video that adds 60 seconds, since that video has to download first. Solo Run opens after Route Duel's first 5 levels.

Do I need internet to get new levels?

No. Every level the game will give you is already in the app. Most games have 150 levels on the road, in three level sets, and after level 150 a game carries on by replaying from its level 51; Quilt Pins picks from a bank of 2,000 quilts. New games or levels arrive only through an app update from the App Store or Google Play.

Can an ad still show when I'm offline?

Sometimes, yes. The app loads the next ad ahead of time, so if one finished downloading before you lost signal, it can still play after a level, once. After that no new ad can arrive until you are back online. We say this plainly because "turn on airplane mode" is often given as a way to stop ads completely, and that isn't quite true.

Can I play Puzzle Jar abroad without paying for roaming?

Yes. Switch off data roaming, or the whole connection, and the game carries on exactly the same, so you are never charged abroad just to play. Your progress stays on the phone, and you can return to wifi at the hotel whenever you like to restore purchases or watch a rewarded video for a free helper.

Does offline play work the same on Android and iPhone?

Yes. The iPhone and Android versions hold the same levels and play the same games, and both save to the phone. The one difference is the first-launch privacy question: on iPhone it is Apple's "Allow tracking?" box, which does not need a connection, while Google's consent form for Europe and the UK needs internet on both phones.

Why do some "offline" puzzle games still need internet?

Usually because they load something from a server before a level can start: an ad that has to play first, an energy timer checked online, a live event, or levels that download as you reach them. When the server can't be reached, the game stops or keeps asking for wifi. We built Puzzle Jar the other way round: levels ship inside the app, and an ad that can't load is simply left out, so no server has to answer before you can play.

Does the jar fill when I play offline?

Yes. Every win in any game drops a piece into the jar on Home, online or offline, and 10 pieces fill it. A full jar gives +1 hint, +1 take back and +1 rescue for any game, then a fresh jar begins. It is the only way to earn free helpers without a connection, which makes it handy on a long trip.
