Snug Skeins is the yarn sort game in Puzzle Jar. Little felt animals peek out of balls of wool, each wound in several layers. Tap a toy and its outer layer unwinds into the wicker basket of that color; if no such basket is out yet, the skein waits in the sewing tin. Fill and send away every basket to win. There is no clock and no move count.

How do you play Snug Skeins?

Tap any toy. Its outer layer of wool flies off as a skein. Two baskets sit on the shelf at a time and each holds three skeins; if one of them matches the skein's color, it lands there. Otherwise it drops into a spot in the sewing tin. A full basket lifts away, the next one comes out, and it collects any skeins of its color from the tin.

What do the numbers and small balls on the toys mean?

The number is how many layers of wool that toy has left. The small ball next to it is the color of the next layer, the one you will uncover after this tap. Reading that second color is the key to the game: a tap that looks safe now might expose a color you have no basket for.

Why did my tap get refused in Snug Skeins?

Because the skein had nowhere to go: no open basket of its color and no free spot in the sewing tin. The game refuses that tap with a short word rather than ending the level. Look for a toy whose outer color matches a basket that is already out.

How do you lose in Snug Skeins?

Only when no tap is left: every toy's next skein would need a tin spot and the tin is full. Then the toys shake, the tin flashes red, and you can rescue, take back, try again or go home. Because a bad tap is refused first, you rarely lose by accident; you lose by filling the tin with colors that are a long way off.

What is the sewing tin for?

It is your spare space: 4 to 7 spots, shown in a row under the baskets. Skeins wait there until a basket of their color comes out. The tin is the whole puzzle in Snug Skeins. Every skein you park there is a spot you cannot use again until its basket arrives.

What are tied toys and pinned toys?

Both are toys you cannot tap yet. A tied toy, from level 8, wears a red bow with a strand of wool running to another toy; it opens once that other toy is bare. A pinned toy, from level 40, has a gold safety pin that comes out once the basket marked with a gold pin is filled and gone.

What are hidden wool and thick wool?

Hidden wool, from level 15, shows a "?" instead of the next layer's color, so you only find out what is under it by tapping. Thick wool, from level 25, is a single layer that counts as two skeins at once: it can fill most of a basket in one tap, or take two tin spots.

What is the best strategy for Snug Skeins?

Tap toys whose outer color goes straight into a basket, and among those, prefer the ones whose next color is also a basket that is out or coming soon. The next three baskets are shown on the shelf. Leave toys with far-off colors alone. If you must use the tin, park colors whose basket is next in line, not the ones at the back of the queue.

What does the Snug Skeins hint do?

It points to the next tap of a winning order that the solver worked out from where you stand. That order is remembered, so a player who keeps following hints will finish the level without the tin ever overflowing.

What does the Snug Skeins rescue give you?

Three helps at once. The tin gets two extra spots, one more basket comes out on the shelf, and the next basket of the color with the most skeins waiting in the tin comes out right away and takes them. That usually clears most of the tin in a single step. You can use it up to twice a level.

How hard does Snug Skeins get?

Level 1 has three toys in two colors with a glowing guide, and cannot be lost. Levels grow to as many as 12 toys with up to 7 layers each. Hard and boss levels shuffle the basket order much more, so the color you need is often several baskets away. Big baskets that hold six skeins arrive at level 60.

Is Snug Skeins a wool sort or a yarn knitting game?

It is the unwind kind of yarn game. You do not knit a picture or feed spools onto a belt; you unwind layers off toys and sort the wool into baskets by color. If you like color sorting with a little planning ahead, and a cozy look, it is a good fit.
