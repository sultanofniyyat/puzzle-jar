Nut Sort is the nuts and bolts sorting game in Puzzle Jar and the fourth game on the level road. Painted hex nuts sit mixed up on steel bolts; you move them between bolts until all four nuts of each color share one bolt and it gets a cap. What sets it apart is that the bolts are not all the same height. There is no clock and no move limit.

How do you play Nut Sort?

Tap a bolt to lift its top nut, then tap another bolt to drop it. A nut can go onto a nut of the same color or onto an empty bolt, never onto a different color. Every color has exactly four nuts, and when all four stand alone on one bolt, a cap twists on and that bolt is done. Cap every color to win.

Why are some bolts in Nut Sort taller or shorter?

To change how much room you have. An ordinary bolt holds four nuts, a tall bolt holds five, and a short helper bolt only two or three. You can tell them apart by the small notches at the foot of each bolt, one per space, with no numbers. From level 7 the mix changes from round to round: some boards are plain, some have a tall bolt, a short helper or both.

Can a short bolt finish a color in Nut Sort?

No. A color needs all four of its nuts on one bolt, and a short helper bolt holds at most three, so it can never get a cap. Think of it as a parking spot: a safe place to put two or three nuts out of the way while you dig out what is underneath. It is still worth a lot, so try not to block it with a nut you have no plan for.

Why did only some of my nuts move?

The bolt you dropped on ran out of room. A move lifts the top nut together with any nuts of the same color directly below it, and then puts as many as fit on the target bolt; the rest stay behind. Dark nuts never join that group, so a run of color stops at the first dark nut under it.

What are dark nuts in Nut Sort?

Charcoal steel nuts with no color symbol, whose real color is hidden until they become the top nut on their bolt. Then the dark coat flakes off and you see what you have. They appear from level 16 on hard and boss levels, and from level 46 on some medium ones. On a dark level every nut under the top one of each bolt starts dark, so the whole board is a guess at first.

What is the best strategy for Nut Sort?

Count the room before every move, not just the colors. Because bolts hold different numbers of nuts, the same move can leave one bolt with a free space or with none. Keep at least one bolt empty or nearly empty as long as you can, use the short helper to park nuts, and dig toward dark nuts early so you learn their colors while you still have space to act on them.

Where should I finish a color in Nut Sort?

On an ordinary four-nut bolt when you have the choice. Once a bolt is capped, it is locked: nothing can be taken off it or added to it. If you cap a color on the tall bolt, its fifth space is locked away with it, and you lose the board's extra room. Use the tall bolt for juggling and let the plain bolts take the caps.

What happens when there are no moves left in Nut Sort?

The game shows "No moves left!" and offers what still works: an extra empty bolt, a take back to step out of the dead end, starting the level again, or going home. Moving a single color's whole stack to an empty bolt of the same height changes nothing, so the game does not count it as a way out.

What does +1 Bolt do in Nut Sort?

It adds one empty bolt with room for four nuts, enough to finish a whole color on it. You get one per level, and the button stays in the bottom bar the whole time. Pay for it either by watching an optional video or with one rescue from the count shared by all 20 games.

How do hints and take backs work in Nut Sort?

A hint lifts the right bolt and puts a gold ring around the bolt to drop on, a safe move found by our solver; it uses one shared hint. Take back undoes your last move, and the first three on every level are free, then each uses one from your count. Unlike Sweet Stacks, where undo costs nothing, take backs here are limited unless you own Remove Ads.

How is Nut Sort different from Bolts & Jars?

They share a hardware look but are different puzzles. In Bolts & Jars you take screws out of stacked plates and send them away into jars, so the board empties and the question is which screw to take next. In Nut Sort nothing leaves the board: every nut stays on a bolt and you rearrange them, so the question is where to put each one.

How is Nut Sort different from Sweet Stacks?

Both are color sorts, but Sweet Stacks uses jars that all hold four, while Nut Sort mixes four-, five- and two- or three-space bolts, so room becomes part of the puzzle. Hidden pieces also arrive sooner here, from level 16 rather than 31, and the board's shape keeps changing. Sweet Stacks has free unlimited undo; Nut Sort gives three free take backs a level.

How hard does Nut Sort get?

Level 1 has three colors and cannot be lost, and levels 1 to 5 are easy or medium. Colors build up to a maximum of 10, each with its own symbol on the nut and the cap.
