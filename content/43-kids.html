Ratings, ads, purchases and privacy description: Puzzle Jar's age ratings, ads and purchases, what data is collected and by whom, and the store settings that stop a child buying by accident. h1: Kids, families, safety and privacy related: is-puzzle-jar-free, help, about ---

Puzzle Jar is a calm, general-audience puzzle app with no chat, no strangers and no account. It is rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play, but it is not a kids' app: it shows ads from Google AdMob and offers in-app purchases, which parents can lock with the store's own settings. We ask for no name, email or photos; Google collects device data for ads, and on Android the game also uses Google Analytics for Firebase.

What age rating does Puzzle Jar have?

4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play. The games have no violence, scary content, gambling or rude words: you sort colors, match shapes and untangle strings. The rating covers what is inside the game. It does not cover the ads, which come from Google's ad network, or the in-app purchases, which the store pages also list.

Is Puzzle Jar safe for kids?

The games themselves are, and there is no way for a child to talk to anyone. But Puzzle Jar is made for a general audience, not as a children's app. It shows ads between levels and has in-app purchases, so for a young child we suggest turning on the store's purchase protection and, if ads are a worry, buying Remove Ads or letting them play offline. Our privacy policy says plainly that the app is not directed at children under 13.

Can kids chat with strangers in Puzzle Jar?

No. Puzzle Jar has no chat, no messages, no friends list, no profiles and no online play with other people. Route Duel's rival is the computer, played by the phone itself. Nobody can see what your child is doing in the game or contact them through it.

Can kids make in-app purchases by accident?

Not without the store's own confirm step. Every purchase opens Apple's or Google's own payment sheet, and nothing is charged until someone confirms it there. Whether that step also asks for a password, Face ID or fingerprint depends on the phone's store settings, and a phone that recently passed a check may not ask again. That is why the settings below are worth turning on.

How do I turn on Ask to Buy on iPhone?

Ask to Buy is part of Apple's Family Sharing. The family organizer turns it on for a child's account in the Family part of iPhone Settings. After that, when the child taps a purchase in any app, Puzzle Jar included, a request goes to the parent's phone to approve or decline. Screen Time's content restrictions can also block in-app purchases altogether.

How do I require a password for purchases on Google Play?

In the Google Play app, open Settings from your profile picture, go to the authentication section and set purchases to need authentication every time. Play then asks for your password or fingerprint before each purchase. Parents can also manage a child's account with Google Family Link.

What kind of ads might my child see in Puzzle Jar?

Ads chosen by Google's AdMob network, often for other apps and games. We do not make or hand-pick them, so we can't promise every one suits a young child. Full-screen ads only show between levels, after a 5-second warning card, and not until the end of the third finished level. If you see an ad that seems wrong for children, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with what it showed; AdMob lets us block that advertiser.

How can my child play Puzzle Jar without ads?

There are two ways. Buy Remove Ads once ($5.99 in the US, shown in your currency), which turns off all full-screen ads for good on that store account; rewarded videos remain, but only play if someone taps "watch ad". Or let your child play with the phone in airplane mode: the games all work, and new ads can't download. Our money and ads page explains both in detail.

What data does Puzzle Jar collect?

We, the studio, collect no personal information: no account, no name, email, contacts, photos or microphone. Your progress stays on the phone. Google's AdMob, which shows the ads, collects device data: the advertising ID, device type and settings, an approximate location from the IP address, how ads are used, and crash and diagnostic details. On Android only, Google Analytics for Firebase also records game events. The full lists are in our iPhone privacy policy and Android privacy policy.

Does Puzzle Jar ask for my child's name or email?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no form anywhere in the app. Puzzle Jar never asks for a name, birthday, email address, phone number or photo, and it does not ask for permission to use the camera, microphone, contacts or GPS location. You open it and play.

What is the "Allow tracking?" question on iPhone?

It is Apple's own question, asked once at the first launch before any ad loads. If you tap "Allow", Google may use the phone's advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", ads still show, but they are less personalized. The game works exactly the same either way. You can change the answer later in iPhone Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Does Puzzle Jar use analytics?

On Android, yes; on iPhone, no. The Android version sends a few events to Google Analytics for Firebase: a won level, milestones at 5, 10, 20 and 50 wins, the value of a purchase, and ad income recorded by Google's ad code. We use them to see whether our Google Ads bring real players. The iPhone version has this switched off.

What are the "Privacy choices" in Settings?

In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows a consent form at the first launch asking how your data may be used for ads. The "Privacy choices" button in Puzzle Jar's Settings opens that form again, so you can change your answers at any time. Outside those regions the button does not appear, because the form is not used there.

Does Puzzle Jar know where I live?

Not from us. The app does not ask for GPS or location permission and has no use for it. Google's ad network does work out a rough area, such as a country or city, from the internet address of the connection, to pick ads in the right language and region. On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase also works out a rough area the same way, and we only see it as totals, such as how many players came from each country.

How do I delete my child's Puzzle Jar data?

Delete the app. Everything the game itself keeps, from progress and streaks to helpers and settings, lives only on the phone, so removing the app removes it. We hold no account or server copy of progress; the only data we see are, on Android, Google Analytics totals that are not tied to a name. Data Google collects for ads is covered by Google's own policies. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the phone's settings; on iPhone, answering "Ask App Not to Track" stops the app from using it.

Can young children play Puzzle Jar without reading?

Mostly, yes. The games are about colors and shapes, with no words, numbers or spelling to solve. The rules cards use short English sentences, so a child who can't read yet may need a grown-up to show the first level; level 1 of each game also has a glowing guide and can't be lost. The menus and buttons are in English only.

Is Puzzle Jar good for color-blind players?

We designed for it. In the sorting and matching games, each color also carries its own mark or shape, such as a heart, star or tick on a screw, so pieces can be matched by mark alone. Other games give each kind of fruit, sea creature or good its own outline. If a color pair still gives you trouble in one game, tell us which.

Is Puzzle Jar good for seniors?

Many of our games suit an unhurried player well. There are no lives and nothing to wait for, 19 of the 20 games have no clock, and every level can be retried for free. Hints show a safe move when you're stuck. Pieces are bright and, in most games, marked by shape as well as color. One honest limit: there's no setting for bigger text, and the app is English only.

Are there timers in Puzzle Jar that rush you?

Only in one mode. Solo Run, inside Route Duel, races a clock: you clear a board alone before the time runs out. Everywhere else there is no countdown. Some games limit moves or pieces instead, which you can plan with at your own pace, and Quilt Pins quietly measures how long a quilt takes only to choose the next one's difficulty, without ever showing a clock.

Does Puzzle Jar have flashing effects?

There are bright effects on a win, such as confetti, fireworks and glowing pieces. Turning on "Reduce motion" in Settings stops the confetti and some glows. It does not remove every animation, because pieces still have to move to be played. If someone in your family is sensitive to flashing light, try a few levels yourself first.

Will Puzzle Jar keep my child playing for hours?

We tried not to build it that way. There are no lives that refill on a timer, no "come back in 2 hours" chests and no push notifications calling you back. There is a day streak, which counts days in a row with a finished level, and a broken streak can be saved with a video, so be aware of that pull. Your phone's own Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing limits work with Puzzle Jar like any app.

Is Puzzle Jar educational?

It's a game first, but it does exercise planning. Most levels reward thinking a few moves ahead, sorting by more than one property, and noticing what blocks what. Quilt Pins is pure logic: every quilt is solved by reasoning, without guessing. We don't make claims beyond that; Puzzle Jar was not designed with teachers or tested in classrooms.

Are puzzle games good for your brain?

Honestly, the research is mixed. Studies of "brain training" often find that people get better at the games they practice, while proof that this improves memory or thinking in daily life is weaker. We don't claim Puzzle Jar makes anyone smarter or prevents memory loss. What it does offer is a quiet challenge that asks you to plan, look and try again.

Do puzzle games help with anxiety?

Some people find that a calm puzzle with no clock helps them settle their mind for a few minutes, and that is the kind of game we set out to make. But a game is not a treatment, and we make no health claims. If anxiety is affecting your daily life, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional.

Can two children share Puzzle Jar on one phone?

They can, but they'll share one set of progress. Puzzle Jar has no profiles, so the level road, the jar, the streak and the helper counts are the same for everyone who plays on that phone. Free play helps a little: each game keeps its own level, so one child can stick to Bubble Picnic and another to Nut Sort.
