Is Puzzle Jar free? Ads, purchases and prices

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Yes. Puzzle Jar is free to download, all 20 games are open from the first launch, and every level can be won without spending anything. The app is paid for by full-screen ads between levels, starting after your 3rd finished level, each one announced by a 5-second "Ad loading" card. A one-time Remove Ads purchase ($5.99 in the US) turns them off for good, and small packs of hints, take backs and rescues are for sale.

Is Puzzle Jar free?#

Yes, free to download on iPhone and Android, with nothing locked. You get all 20 games, the daily puzzles and the full level road without paying. There is no subscription and no trial that runs out. The game earns money in two ways: ads between levels, and optional purchases such as Remove Ads and helper packs. You can play for months and never open the shop.

Why does Puzzle Jar have ads?#

Because the ads are what keep the game free. We are a small studio, and most players never buy anything, so the ads between levels pay for the new games and levels we keep adding. We chose ads over a paywall or a waiting timer: there is never a clock standing between you and the next level. If ads bother you, one purchase removes them instead of a monthly fee.

When do ads start in Puzzle Jar?#

At the end of your third finished level. The first two levels you finish, in any of the 20 games, never show a full-screen ad, so you can try the app properly first. On iPhone, the app also asks Apple's "Allow tracking?" question at the very first launch, before any ad is loaded. From the end of that third level on, an ad can appear whenever a level ends.

How often do ads show between levels?#

Once 3 levels are finished, a full-screen ad can come at the end of any level, won or lost, when you tap to go on. There are two breaks: no full-screen ad comes within 20 seconds of a rewarded video you chose to watch, and none comes if no ad has loaded yet. In that second case you simply go on to the next screen without an ad.

Are there ads in the middle of a level?#

No. A level is never stopped by an ad. Full-screen ads only appear after a level is over, on the way to the next one or back home. If you leave a level halfway through with the back button, no ad plays at all. The only videos you can see during play are rewarded ones that you start yourself by tapping a "watch ad" button.

Does Puzzle Jar have banner ads?#

No. There are no banner ads anywhere in Puzzle Jar: not along the bottom of the board, not on the Home screen, not in Settings. The board always has the whole screen. The only ads are the full-screen ones between levels and the rewarded videos you pick yourself.

What is the 5-second "Ad loading" screen?#

It is a warning card we show before every full-screen ad. It counts down from 5, so an ad never jumps out at you in the middle of a tap. On the card is a REMOVE ADS button with the price in your currency. If you ignore it, the countdown ends and the ad plays; if you buy, the ad is dropped there and then.

What do rewarded videos give you?#

One helper each time you watch to the end. On the offer sheet a video gives +1 hint, +1 take back or +1 rescue, whichever you asked for. In most games the first rescue of a level can be paid with a video. In Solo Run one video a run adds 60 seconds to the clock, and on Home a video can save a broken day streak of 2 days or more, that day or the next.

Do I have to watch rewarded videos?#

No, they are always your choice. A rewarded video only starts when you tap a button that says "watch ad", and saying "NOT NOW" costs nothing. If you close a video early you just don't get that reward. Nothing in the game, not a level, a game or a feature, is locked until you watch one.

How much does Remove Ads cost?#

$5.99 in the US, paid once. It is not a subscription: you buy it once and keep it, and it comes back on a new phone through Restore purchases. Outside the US the App Store or Google Play shows its own price in your currency, which can be higher or lower than the US figure.

What does Remove Ads remove?#

It removes every full-screen ad between levels, in all 20 games, along with the 5-second "Ad loading" card. What stays are the rewarded videos, because those are a way to earn free helpers: the "watch ad" buttons are still there if you want a free hint or rescue, and they never play by themselves. We think that is fairer than taking the free option away from paying players.

What else do you get with Remove Ads?#

Unlimited take backs. Normally the first 3 take backs on each level are free and later ones come from your count; with Remove Ads every take back is free, and the button shows an infinity mark instead of a number. We added this because a common complaint about puzzle games is that paying to remove ads still leaves undoing a slip behind a paywall. Hints and rescues still come from their counts.

How do I remove ads from Puzzle Jar?#

Buy Remove Ads in one of two places. On the 5-second "Ad loading" card, tap REMOVE ADS. Or open Settings with the button next to the name on the Home screen and tap "Remove ads", which shows the price. Apple or Google then asks you to confirm. Once it goes through, the Settings button says ads are removed, and the "Ad loading" card never appears again.

Can I get rid of ads without paying?#

Not with a switch, but playing with no internet stops most of them. Full-screen ads have to download before they can play, so in airplane mode they mostly cannot load and the level ends without one. An ad that loaded before you went offline can still show once. The catch: rewarded videos need internet too, so offline you cannot earn free helpers that way. More on this on our offline play page.

Why are Puzzle Jar's prices different in my country?#

Because each store sells in local currency. The App Store and Google Play turn a price into your currency using their own price points, and in many countries that price includes sales tax or VAT, so it rarely matches the US figure after exchange. The game shows the price your store gives it, so the number on a button is the store's price for your account. The US prices on this page are only a guide.

What is the Starter Pack?#

A one-time deal: Remove Ads, 30 hints and 5 rescues for $2.99 in the US, less than Remove Ads costs on its own. It appears once, on the Home screen, after you win level 21 of the level road. From the moment you first see it, it stays on sale for 48 hours, with a small timer on Home. It is never shown to anyone who already owns Remove Ads.

Can I still get the Starter Pack after it ends?#

No. When its 48 hours run out, the Starter Pack is gone for good and does not come back later. We did it this way so the offer is honest: a one-time deal that really is one-time, instead of a "last chance" that returns every week. Remove Ads and all the helper packs stay on sale at their normal prices.

Is Puzzle Jar pay to win?#

No. Puzzle Jar has no scores against other people, no rankings and no levels that need a purchase. Hints and rescues make a hard level quicker, but they are never required: you can retry a level as often as you like at no cost. A player who never pays sees exactly the same levels as one who buys everything.

Can every level be beaten without paying?#

Yes. Money buys speed in Puzzle Jar, never access: helpers save you tries, but nothing you can buy opens a level, adds a level or changes how a level is built. A player who has spent nothing has every move a paying player has. A hard level may take several tries; it never takes a purchase. How we prove each level can be won is on our stuck and difficulty page.

Are any games or levels locked behind a purchase?#

No. All 20 games are open in Free play from the first launch, and the level road and daily puzzles cost nothing. The only thing in the app that unlocks by playing is Solo Run, the timed mode inside Route Duel, which opens at Route Duel level 6. Nothing can be unlocked early with money.

Does Puzzle Jar have coins or gems?#

No. There is no coin, gem or other in-game money to earn, collect or buy. Every price in the shop is in real money, shown in your own currency, and each pack says plainly what it gives, such as "10 hints". We left out an in-game currency on purpose, so you never have to work out what a bundle of gems is really worth.

Does Puzzle Jar have lives or energy?#

No. There are no lives, no energy bar and no waiting to play. Lose a level and you can tap TRY AGAIN, as many times as you want, for free; the only thing that may come first is a full-screen ad, unless you own Remove Ads. A few games, such as Quilt Pins and Pencil Case, have 3 hearts inside a single level, but losing them only ends that try.

Which hint pack is the best value?#

The 150-hint pack, if you use hints often. In the US it costs $9.99, about 6.7 cents a hint, against 9.9 cents in the 10-hint pack and about 8.3 cents in the 60-hint pack. If you only want a few for one stuck level, the $0.99 pack is the cheaper buy overall. Before you buy, remember the free supply: a full jar on Home gives +1 hint, and a rewarded video gives +1.

Do hints, take backs and rescues expire?#

No, they have no expiry date. A hint bought today still works in a year, in any of the 20 games. There is one catch: helper counts are saved only on your phone. If you delete the app or move to a new phone, unused hints, take backs and rescues are lost, and Restore purchases cannot bring packs back because each pack is used up as you spend it.

How do I restore purchases in Puzzle Jar?#

Open Settings from the Home screen and tap "Restore purchases". You need internet and the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Restore brings back one-time purchases: Remove Ads, the Remove Ads part of the Starter Pack, and the old unlimited hints or take backs from Route Duel. It does not refill used or lost packs of hints, take backs or rescues.

Why do I still see ads after buying Remove Ads?#

First, check which kind of ad it is. Rewarded videos stay after the purchase, but they only play when you tap a "watch ad" button. If a full-screen ad or the "Ad loading" card still appears on its own, the purchase has not reached the game: open Settings, tap "Restore purchases" while online, and make sure the phone is signed in to the account that paid. If it still happens, email us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com.

How do I get a refund for a Puzzle Jar purchase?#

Ask the store you paid, because Apple and Google handle every payment and we never see your card. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase and choose to request a refund. On Android, start from Google Play's refund policy help page, which links to the request steps. The store decides; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

What if an ad in Puzzle Jar looks misleading or inappropriate?#

Please tell us. The ads come from Google's AdMob network, so we do not make or pick each one, but AdMob lets us block an advertiser or a whole category once we know about it. Email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with what the ad showed, which app or game it was for, and roughly when you saw it. A screenshot helps.

Does every Puzzle Jar purchase go through Apple or Google?#

Yes. Every purchase opens the App Store's or Google Play's own payment sheet, and nothing is charged until you confirm it there. Depending on your store settings, that step can also ask for a password, Face ID or fingerprint. That also means the store's family settings apply, such as Ask to Buy; our kids and safety page explains them for parents.

I bought unlimited hints in Route Duel. Do I still have them?#

Yes. Puzzle Jar grew out of Route Duel, and the old "unlimited hints" and "unlimited take backs" purchases are still honored in all 20 games. They are no longer sold, but if you own one, hints or take backs show an infinity mark and cost nothing. On a new phone, Restore purchases brings them back like Remove Ads.

Are there cheats or promo codes for Puzzle Jar?#

No. We don't run promo codes, and there are no cheat codes in the game. Sites that offer "free hint generators" for puzzle games are not connected to us and cannot add anything to your phone; avoid giving them your details. The free ways to get helpers are the real ones: fill the jar with 10 wins for one hint, one take back and one rescue, or watch an optional video for one more.
