Balloon Bunch: the floating balloon pop game

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Balloon Bunch is the tap-to-blast game in Puzzle Jar where the pieces float. Tap two or more touching balloons of one color to pop them; the balloons below drift up into the gap and new ones rise in from the bottom of the tent. Reach the goals at the top before your moves run out. A bunch of 5 or more leaves a firework, and fireworks that touch join into a bigger one by themselves.

How do you play Balloon Bunch?#

Tap a bunch: two or more balloons of the same color that touch. They pop, the ones under them float up to fill the space, and new balloons rise in from below. Each pop costs one move, and the counters at the top go down as you collect the colors they ask for. Every color wears its own picture (red heart, blue star, yellow smile, green clover, orange paw, purple moon), so you never have to tell colors apart by shade alone.

Why do the balloons move around after I pop some?#

Because they really float. The balloons are not locked in a grid: they rise, bump into each other and settle under the tent roof, much like real balloons. So a pop can push balloons into new bunches you didn't plan, for better or worse. A move ends when everything has stopped drifting, which takes a couple of seconds at most.

How do fireworks work in Balloon Bunch?#

Pop 5 to 7 balloons at once for a small firework, 8 to 10 for a medium one, 11 or more for a big one. The firework stays where you tapped the bunch. Tap it and it pops every balloon around it, reaching wider the bigger it is, and sets off any other firework it touches, all for a single move.

What happens when two fireworks touch?#

They join into one firework a size bigger, all on their own, as the balloons drift. Two small ones become a medium, and two mediums become a big one. This is the heart of the game: make two fireworks close to each other, let the floating bring them together, and you get a bigger blast without paying for it in extra pops.

Should I always go for the biggest firework?#

No. A big firework needs 11 balloons in one bunch, and chasing it can burn several moves on small pops. We measured a player that always taps the biggest bunch and sets off every firework straight away: it won only 6 of our 37 hard levels and 1 of 12 boss levels. Firework placement and timing matter more than size.

How do you win and lose in Balloon Bunch?#

Win by bringing every goal counter at the top to zero; lose if your moves run out first. Levels give between 12 and 34 moves. Tapping a balloon with no match beside it does nothing and uses no move, so there is no penalty for a stray tap.

Where should I tap first in Balloon Bunch?#

Think about what will rise into the gap. Balloons move up, so popping a bunch high in the tent pulls up everything under it, while a pop near the bottom only changes the lower rows and the new balloons coming in. Pop next to the clouds and tied balloons you need to clear, and pick bunches whose neighbors, once they float up, will form a bunch in a goal color.

What are clouds and dark clouds?#

Obstacles that sit in the tent and get in the balloons' way. A cloud (from level 4) breaks when balloons touching it pop; a dark cloud (from level 14) needs two hits. Because everything floats up, a cloud keeps the balloons beneath it from rising past, so breaking one often reshapes the board.

What is a tied balloon?#

A balloon wrapped in ribbon, from level 8. It can't be tapped and can't be part of a bunch until a balloon touching it pops and unties it. After that it plays like any other balloon of its color, so untying the ones in a goal color early gives you more to work with.

How does the Balloon Bunch hint choose a tap?#

It shows the tap our careful player would make. To choose it, the game takes the seven taps that look best, plays each one out with the real floating, then tries the best four taps after each. If you wait 6 seconds without tapping, the biggest bunch on the board glows for free.

What does a rescue give you in Balloon Bunch?#

Five extra moves. Two are allowed per level, and one of them can be earned by watching a video instead of spending a rescue you own.

Can you get through Balloon Bunch by tapping anything?#

Only on the early levels. The balloons that rise in are the same on every try of a level, and when no bunch is left they swap colors the same way each time, so careful play is what gets you through, not luck.

How tough are the boss levels in Balloon Bunch?#

Tough by design: a boss comes every fourth hard level, and most players need more than one try or a rescue on them. If you win easily, the game moves you to a tougher level set. Levels come in rounds of easy, medium and hard, so a breather always follows. The start is gentle: level 1 asks for 14 blue stars in 31 moves and can't be lost.
