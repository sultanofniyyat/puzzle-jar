Help: phones, progress, settings and support

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Puzzle Jar runs on iPhone with iOS 15 or newer and on phones with Android 7.0 or newer. Your progress is saved only on the phone: there is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress, while Restore purchases brings back Remove Ads. For anything not answered here, email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with your phone model and the version number from Settings.

Which iPhones can run Puzzle Jar?#

Any iPhone with iOS 15 or newer. The game runs full screen, in portrait, and the board fits itself to the screen size. An iPhone that is stuck on iOS 14 or earlier can't install it. To check your version, open Settings, then General, then About, and look at iOS Version.

Is there an iPad version of Puzzle Jar?#

No. Puzzle Jar is built for iPhone only, and we don't make an iPad version at the moment. iPads can sometimes run iPhone apps in a phone-sized window, but we don't test Puzzle Jar that way, so we can't promise how it looks or plays. If an iPad version would matter to you, tell us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com; it helps us decide what to build next.

Does Puzzle Jar work on iPhone Duo?#

Yes. Puzzle Jar supports both screens of iPhone Duo, the outer one you use when the phone is closed and the bigger inner one when it is open, and fills whichever one you are using. The game plays in portrait on both, with the same levels and the same saved progress, since it is one app on one phone.

Which Android phones can run Puzzle Jar?#

Phones with Android 7.0 or newer, which covers most Android phones in use today. It's built for both 32-bit (ARMv7) and 64-bit (ARM64) processors, so older budget phones are included. If Google Play says your device isn't compatible, it is most often because the phone has an older Android version.

Does Puzzle Jar work on Android tablets?#

Possibly, but we can't promise it. We haven't blocked tablets, but we design and test Puzzle Jar on phones, in portrait, so on a large tablet the layout may look stretched or leave empty space. If Google Play offers it on your tablet, you're welcome to try it. Please tell us how it looks.

How big is the Puzzle Jar download?#

On iPhone, about 106 MB. On Android, Google Play shows the exact size for your phone on the install page, because Android downloads are built for each phone. After that, no extra level packs are downloaded: all 20 games and their levels are in that first download, and later changes arrive as normal app updates.

What languages is Puzzle Jar in?#

The app itself is in English only. The games need very little reading, since they are about colors and shapes, but the rules cards, buttons and messages are in English. The App Store page is written in several languages, so the store description you see may be in your own, even though the game inside is English.

Do I need an account to play Puzzle Jar?#

No. There is no sign-up, login, email or password, and no Facebook or Google sign-in. You download it and play. Purchases are handled by your Apple ID or Google account, the same as any app, but Puzzle Jar never asks you to create anything of its own.

Where is my Puzzle Jar progress saved?#

On your phone, inside the app's own storage. Each finished level, your road level, every game's own level, the jar, the day streak, your hints, take backs and rescues, and your settings are written to the phone straight away. We keep no copy on a server, which is why the game works offline and why we never need an account from you.

Does Puzzle Jar have cloud save?#

No, there is no cloud save, no iCloud or Google sync and no Game Center. That's the downside of not having accounts: we can't move or recover your progress, because we don't store it anywhere. Only purchases are kept by the store, and you get them back with Restore purchases.

What happens to my progress if I delete Puzzle Jar?#

It is deleted with the app. Your levels, streak, jar and any unused hints, take backs and rescues are lost, and reinstalling starts you again at level 1 with the welcome gift. One-time purchases are safe: after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in Settings. If you only want to free up space, check first whether there is another way, such as offloading other apps.

How do I move my Puzzle Jar progress to a new phone?#

The game can't move it for you, because it has no account or cloud save. On the new phone you start again from level 1, then tap Restore purchases in Settings to get back Remove Ads and other one-time purchases. A full phone-to-phone transfer or backup restore made by Apple or Google may bring app data across, but we haven't tested that, so please don't count on it.

Can I play on an iPhone and an Android phone with the same progress?#

No. Each phone keeps its own progress, and nothing is shared between them. Purchases don't cross over either: something bought on the App Store belongs to your Apple ID, and something bought on Google Play belongs to your Google account. Two iPhones on the same Apple ID can each restore Remove Ads, but each still has its own levels.

What happens if I leave a level halfway?#

You start that level again next time. Tapping back during a level asks "Leave this level?", and leaving throws away the moves made on that try. Helpers already spent on it are not refunded, but you lose no level, no streak day and no money, and no ad plays. Your road and game levels stay where they were.

Why does Puzzle Jar crash or not open?#

Try these in order: close the app fully and open it again; restart the phone; update Puzzle Jar from the store; make sure the phone has some free storage; update the phone's system if an update is waiting. Please don't delete and reinstall as a first step, because that erases your progress. If it still crashes, email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with your phone model, its iOS or Android version, the Puzzle Jar version and what you were doing.

Why isn't my Puzzle Jar purchase going through?#

Usually it's the store connection or the store account, not the game. Check that you have internet, that you're signed in to the App Store or Google Play, and that your payment method there works. Purchase restrictions such as Ask to Buy or Screen Time can also stop it. If money was taken but nothing arrived, tap Restore purchases in Settings; for a hint pack, write to us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with the store receipt.

What does "Couldn't reach the App Store" mean?#

It means Puzzle Jar couldn't get an answer from the store, so nothing was bought or restored. On Android the message says Google Play instead. The usual causes are no internet, airplane mode, or the store being briefly unavailable. Connect to wifi or mobile data, wait a moment and tap the button again. Your purchases are safe with the store in the meantime.

How do I turn off sound or music in Puzzle Jar?#

Open Settings from the Home screen. Sound and Music are separate switches: Sound controls the taps, pops and win jingles, and Music controls the background tune. Turn off one, the other or both. The choice is remembered for all 20 games, and the phone's own volume buttons work as normal too.

How do I turn off vibration in Puzzle Jar?#

Switch off Vibration in Settings. The game uses short buzzes as feedback, for example when you get stuck on a level; with the switch off, the phone stays still. The one switch covers all 20 games, and the choice is kept until you change it.

What does Reduce motion do in Puzzle Jar?#

It calms the screen down. With Reduce motion on, win confetti is turned off and some glowing halos and extra effects are skipped, while the pieces still move as they must for the game to work. It is useful if busy movement is tiring or distracting. You'll find the switch in Settings, under Vibration.

How do I see a game's rules again?#

Tap the "?" button while playing. Each game shows its rules automatically the first time you open it, and the "?" button brings the same rules card back whenever you want it, without restarting the level. Each game also has its own page on this site with fuller rules and tips, linked from our home page.

How do I update Puzzle Jar?#

Through your phone's store. On iPhone, open the App Store, tap your profile picture and pull down to refresh the list of updates, then tap Update next to Puzzle Jar. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then the section for managing your apps and device, and update it there. Updating keeps your progress; only deleting the app removes it.

Where do I find my Puzzle Jar version number?#

At the bottom of Settings. Open Settings from the Home screen and you'll see "Puzzle Jar" followed by the version number, above the "Done" button. Please include it when you write to us about a problem, because fixes often arrive in a specific version.

How do I reset my progress in Puzzle Jar?#

There is no reset button in the app. The only way to start over is to delete Puzzle Jar and install it again, which clears all progress, streaks and unused helpers. Remove Ads and other one-time purchases come back with Restore purchases; hint, take back and rescue packs do not. If you only want to replay a game, Free play and the daily puzzles may do instead.

How do I leave a review for Puzzle Jar?#

On the store page. On iPhone, open Puzzle Jar's App Store page, scroll to Ratings & Reviews and tap the option to write a review. On Android, open the Google Play page and tap a star under "Rate this app". Honest reviews, good or bad, help other players and tell us what to fix.

How do I contact Puzzle Jar support?#

Email us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com. We are a small studio and read every message ourselves. To get a useful answer faster, include your phone model, its iOS or Android version, the Puzzle Jar version from the bottom of Settings, which game and level, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

I played Route Duel. What happened to my levels?#

They're still yours. Route Duel became Puzzle Jar in the 2.0.0 update on the same store page, so updating kept your Route Duel levels, Remove Ads and other purchases. Hints and take backs you had saved were moved into Puzzle Jar's shared counts, and every player also got the welcome gift on top.

Where is Route Duel now?#

Inside Puzzle Jar, as one of its 20 games. Open Free play on the Home screen and tap Route Duel to carry on your duels against the computer rival, or play Solo Run from the same card. On the level road, Route Duel comes last in each lap of the 20 games. Its own page explains the rules.

Does Puzzle Jar send notifications?#

No. Puzzle Jar doesn't send push notifications or reminders, and it never asks for permission to. You won't get "your lives are full" or "come back" messages. Anything the app has to tell you, such as the ad warning card or the Starter Pack offer, appears only while you have it open.
