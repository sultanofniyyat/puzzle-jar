Bubble Picnic: the bubble shooter

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Bubble Picnic is the bubble shooter in Puzzle Jar. Touch and slide to aim, then let go to shoot; when it lands in a touching group of three or more of its color, counting itself, they pop, and anything left hanging falls. Each level gives you a set number of bubbles and one goal: clear every bubble, or free the gold stars. There is no timer and no ceiling coming down on you.

How do you aim in Bubble Picnic?#

Touch the board and slide your finger. A dotted path shows the whole flight, bounces included, and a ring marks the exact spot where the bubble will stick. Let go to shoot. Bubbles bounce off the side walls, so you can bank a shot around a cluster that is in the way and reach a gap you could not hit straight on.

When do bubbles pop and when do they fall?#

Three or more touching bubbles of one color pop, counting the one you just shot. After the pop, every bubble that is no longer linked to the top of the field drops off the board. Falling bubbles count toward clearing the field just like popped ones, which is why cutting a thin link can be worth far more than a big pop.

Can I swap the bubble I'm about to shoot?#

Yes, and it's free. Tap the two bubbles at the bottom to switch the one in the shooter with the next one. You can always see both, so every shot is really a choice between two colors. Swapping costs no bubble and you can do it as often as you like.

How do you get a rainbow bubble?#

Clear 8 or more bubbles with one shot, adding the popped and the fallen together. Your next bubble then turns into a rainbow bubble, which counts as any color when it lands. Save it for a spot where it touches two different colored pairs, or where one pop will drop a big hanging cluster.

How do you win and lose in Bubble Picnic?#

Meet the level's goal, shown at the top and under the field, before your bubbles run out. Early levels ask you to clear every bubble; from level 4 many ask you to free the stars instead. A level gives between 8 and 45 bubbles. When the last one is shot and the goal isn't met, the level is lost.

How do you free the stars?#

A gold star caught in the field is freed when a pop touches it, or when it falls with the bubbles around it. The second way is often cheaper: instead of working through the bubbles next to a star, look for the link that holds its whole cluster to the top and break that. Star levels start with deeper fields, 5 to 8 rows, so there is usually no need to clear everything.

What are stone bubbles?#

Gray stones, from level 8, that never pop, whatever you hit them with. The only way to get rid of one is to make it fall, by clearing the bubbles that hold it to the top. When a stone sits in the middle of the field, plan your shots around the bubbles above it.

What is the best strategy in Bubble Picnic?#

Aim for drops, not just pops. Hitting the few bubbles that hold a big cluster up brings the whole thing down for one shot. Use the free swap to keep the color you will need next, and don't fire a bubble anywhere just to get rid of it: in our tests, stray shots that piled up were the main reason players ran out. Plain sensible play, always taking the shot that clears the most, still won about half of our hard levels.

Are the bubble colors in Bubble Picnic random?#

No. The colors you are given are set by the level and by what is still on the field, so the same level hands you the same bubbles each time you play it. Nothing is changed to make you lose. Each level has been beaten by our careful computer player, and its winning line of shots is saved as proof.

What does a hint do in Bubble Picnic?#

It draws the best shot in gold and rings the two bubbles at the bottom if you should swap first. Our careful player tries every spot it can reach with either bubble, plays out the best shot after each, and shows a straight shot instead of a bank shot when both do the same job. Pause for 6 seconds and the shot that clears the most right now is shown for free.

What happens when Bubble Picnic runs out of bubbles?#

Five more bubbles can be added to your shooter, two times per level at most; a video pays for one of those if you like, otherwise each costs a rescue. Another way out: undo the last shot, which returns that bubble to you, and on each level three of those undos cost nothing.

Can I tell the bubbles apart without relying on color?#

Yes. Every color carries its own white mark: a heart on red, a cloud on blue, a bolt on yellow, a clover on green, a daisy on orange and a moon on purple. You can play the whole game by shape alone, which helps players who find some colors hard to tell apart.

How big do Bubble Picnic fields get?#

Up to 9 bubbles wide and 13 rows deep, with as many as 6 colors. Level 1 is tiny by comparison: 2 rows and 3 colors, 13 bubbles to clear with 45 to shoot, and none of 400 computer test players lost it.

Is Bubble Picnic like the classic bubble shooters?#

Yes. If you grew up on arcade or flash bubble shooters, the aiming and bouncing will feel familiar. The difference is the pace: there is no timer and no ceiling pushing down, so every shot can be lined up calmly, and the challenge comes from having a fixed number of bubbles instead.
