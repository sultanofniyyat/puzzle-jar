About Puzzle Jar and the people who make it

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Puzzle Jar is made by Sultan of Niyyat, a small independent studio. We design the games, build the levels and test them ourselves, and we answer the support email ourselves too. This page explains how we work, why the app looks the way it does, and how to reach us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com.

Who makes Puzzle Jar?#

Sultan of Niyyat, a small independent studio and the only company behind the app. Every one of the 20 games is our own design: its name, its rules, its pieces and its levels. The genres are well known, screw puzzles, nut sorts, bubble shooters and so on, but nothing in Puzzle Jar is licensed from or made with another game company. The picture outlines in Route Duel come from Google's free Noto Emoji set, which we credit in Settings.

Why did you put 20 games in one app?#

Because the most common complaint about single-genre puzzle apps, in the reviews we read, is that the same rule gets repetitive after a while. A collection lets the puzzle change every three levels while your helpers, your jar and your streak stay in one place. Reviews of other game collections also praise having many games without filling up the phone, and we liked the idea of one download, one Remove Ads purchase and one set of settings.

Why was Puzzle Jar called Route Duel before?#

Route Duel was our first game: a duel against a computer rival on picture boards, released in October 2026. In November we decided the app should hold several completely different puzzle games, each with its own look, and a duel-only name no longer fitted, so we picked one that suits a collection. That became update 2.0.0 on the same store pages, so nobody had to download anything new or lose their progress. Route Duel itself is still inside, as the last game on the road.

How do you test that every level can be won?#

A computer solver plays each level before it ships, with no hints, no take backs and no rescues, and a level that the solver cannot finish is thrown away. Then about 400 simulated "casual" players, which mostly make sensible moves and sometimes careless ones, play it too, and we record how many of them get stuck. Automatic tests check every shipped level again before each release, so a rule change cannot quietly break an old level.

How do you decide how hard a level is?#

By that stuck rate rather than by feel. Levels come in rounds of easy, medium and hard, with a boss on every fourth hard level and an easy breather straight after each hard one, and each kind must land in its own band of stuck players. The game also adjusts to you, a step harder after easy wins and a step easier after a struggle. The full detail, with the numbers, is on our page about difficulty and getting stuck.

How did you decide where the ads go?#

We started from what puzzle players complain about most in store reviews, which is ads, and chose the gentlest setup that still keeps the app free: no banners, nothing during a level, no ad until you have finished three levels, and a 5-second notice with a Remove Ads button before each one. Videos that give extra help only play when you tap them. Prices and exactly what Remove Ads changes are on the page about cost and ads.

How did you choose the games in Puzzle Jar?#

We looked for genres that millions of people already enjoy on their phones and that stay calm: sorting, tile matching, block puzzles, jams, merges, blasts, a bubble shooter and a logic grid. Then we kept only games played with shapes and colors. We decided against card, word and number games, so there is no solitaire, no word search and no sudoku. A few early games were later replaced because we liked them less than the rest and their genres were less popular.

Is Puzzle Jar designed for color-blind players?#

Yes, as far as we could manage. In the sorting and matching games every color also carries its own symbol, such as a heart, a shield, a star, a tick, a bolt or an eye, and within a game the same color always has the same symbol, so you can play by shape alone. Other games give each kind of piece its own outline or picture, such as the twelve goods in Shelf Sort or the six fruits in Fruit Tumble.

How can I contact the Puzzle Jar team?#

Email us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com, or use the support page at https://sultanofniyyat.github.io/route-duel-privacy/support/. We read every message ourselves; there is no outside support company and no chatbot. Please include the game and level number if your question is about a level.

How do I report a bug or suggest a new game?#

Send an email to sultanofniyyat@gmail.com. For a bug, tell us the game, the level number, your phone model and the app version, which you can find at the bottom of Settings; a screenshot helps a lot. For a game idea, name the kind of puzzle and what you like about it. Every bug report goes into our numbered bug log, where it stays until it is fixed and checked.

Who wrote this website?#

We did, with help from an AI writing assistant. We gave it the real rules, numbers and code of the game, and then checked every answer against the app and its code before publishing. If you find anything here that does not match what you see in Puzzle Jar, please email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com and we will fix the page.

Is Puzzle Jar connected to the other puzzle games named on this site?#

No. Where we mention other games or genres, it is only to explain what kind of puzzle one of ours is, for readers who already know that style. We have no partnership with any other game maker, none of them made or approved Puzzle Jar, and their names belong to their owners.
