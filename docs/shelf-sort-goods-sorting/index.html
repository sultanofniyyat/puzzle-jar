Shelf Sort: the goods sorting game

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Shelf Sort is the goods sorting game in Puzzle Jar. A wooden cupboard holds small shelves of painted goods, three spots to a shelf. Move goods one at a time onto shelves with a free spot; three of the same kind side by side in front pop. Empty a shelf's front and the row behind steps forward. Clear every shelf to win. There is no timer and no move count; you lose only if every spot fills up.

How do you play Shelf Sort?#

Drag an item onto another shelf that has a free spot, or tap the item and then tap the shelf. When the front of a shelf holds three of the same kind, they pop and leave. Goods come in twelve kinds, each with its own outline, from teacups and teddies to cacti and gift boxes, so you can sort by shape even if colors are hard for you to tell apart.

What are the dimmed goods behind the shelves?#

The next row. Each shelf can hide more goods behind the front one, shown faded so you can plan for them. Little boxes on the plank tell you how many rows still wait there. When you empty a shelf's front, that row slides forward and fills it again.

Should I empty a shelf to reveal the row behind?#

Only when you are ready for what is behind it. Emptying a front shows new goods, which can give you the third item you need. But those goods take up the spots you just freed. Check the dimmed row first: if it brings kinds you can already match, go ahead; if it brings three strangers, clear somewhere else.

How do you lose in Shelf Sort?#

Mainly by filling every spot in the cupboard, so no item has anywhere to go. The card says "No room left", with take back, an extra shelf, a fresh try or home. The game also stops you with a "No way to clear" card if it works out that no win is left from where you are. There is no clock and no count of moves.

What does "Going round in circles?" mean in Shelf Sort?#

It is a friendly check, not a loss. If you make 40 moves in a row without a single pop, the game offers help: take back, +1 shelf, try again, or KEEP TRYING to carry on as you were. We put it in place of the timer many sorting games use, so you are never cut off, just asked.

How do locked shelves open in Shelf Sort?#

By playing. A locked shelf, from level 10, holds goods under a padlock with one to three rings. Each time goods pop anywhere in the cupboard, a ring lights up; when all its rings are lit, the lock bursts open. No locked shelf in this game needs an ad or a payment to open.

What are the shelves with a sign?#

Picky shelves. From level 31 some empty shelves carry a sign showing one kind of goods, and they only take that kind. They are useful for finishing a set but can also leave you short of space. From the same level some kinds come six times, so you may pop the same goods twice.

What is a good Shelf Sort strategy?#

Gather pairs. Move items next to their match before you hunt for the third, and keep at least one shelf with free spots as working space. Look at the dimmed rows when you choose which shelf to empty. When locks are on the board, any pop helps open them, so quick, easy pops come first.

What does the Shelf Sort hint show?#

It lifts one item and rings the shelf to put it on. The move comes from our solver and is safe: following it keeps a win possible. If the solver finds no safe move from where you are, the game says so and keeps your hint, which is your cue to take back a few moves.

What does +1 SHELF do?#

It adds one empty shelf to the cupboard, three new spots to sort into. It is Shelf Sort's rescue and works once per level; the button sits in the bottom bar the whole time and also appears on the "No room left" card. You pay for it with a video you choose to watch or with one rescue from your shared count.

How hard does Shelf Sort get?#

Level 1 cannot be lost by anyone, and the next good move is always lifted and ringed for you. From there, cupboards range from 3 to 7 shelves. The difficulty is mostly in how mixed the goods are: easy levels keep each shelf close to one kind, while boss levels scatter nearly two kinds per shelf, with rows behind, locks and picky shelves.

Why does Shelf Sort have no timer?#

Because players of goods sorting games often complain about strict level timers, and about locked boxes that need an ad to open. So Shelf Sort has neither a timer nor a move count, and its locked shelves open only through play. Every level is checked by computer to be winnable without any help.
