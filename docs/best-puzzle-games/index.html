The best puzzle games for your situation

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

For each need below we give one to three honest picks, from other studios and from our own app, Puzzle Jar, and say where Puzzle Jar is not the right choice (no iPad version, no word or number games, ads unless you buy Remove Ads). Facts about other games come from their official store pages, checked on 2026-11-24.

What are the best offline puzzle games for iPhone?#

Pick games that store every level inside the app. Puzzle Jar does: all 20 games and the daily puzzles play in airplane mode, and it runs on iOS 15 or newer. Paid one-off games such as Monument Valley and Monument Valley 2 (ustwo games) are another route, and Apple Arcade says its games have no ads. For any game that is not ours, open it once in airplane mode at home before you count on it offline. Our offline page explains exactly what in Puzzle Jar still needs internet.

What are the best offline puzzle games for Android?#

On Android, Simon Tatham's Puzzles, ported by Chris Boyle, is a standout: free, open source, and its Google Play page shows no ads label and no purchases label. It holds 40 classic logic puzzles. If you prefer bright color-and-shape games, Puzzle Jar works offline on Android 7.0 or newer, with screw, sort, tile, bus and bubble games in one download. Mini Metro by Dinosaur Polo Club is a paid strategy-puzzle choice with no ads or purchases label on Google Play. We have not tested other studios' games offline, so open them once in airplane mode before relying on them.

What are the best puzzle games for adults?#

Adults usually want depth without childish rewards or pressure. Our picks: Quilt Pins for pure logic (one answer per grid, solved by reasoning), Bolts & Jars for planning a few moves ahead, and, from other studios, Monument Valley for a beautiful paid puzzle story. Simon Tatham's Puzzles suits people who like pencil-and-paper logic. Puzzle Jar was made with adults in mind: no story, no characters to dress, just puzzles that grow harder in steady rounds.

What are the best puzzle games for seniors?#

Good choices are unhurried, readable and forgiving. In Puzzle Jar, start with Sweet Stacks, where undo and restart are always free, or Button Tin, which has no move limit; colors also carry symbols, which helps tired eyes. Honest limits: there is no large-text setting, the app is English only, and on Apple devices it runs on iPhone, not iPad. Many older players also like jigsaws; Apple Arcade lists "Jigsaw Puzzle by MobilityWare+" for subscribers.

What are good brain games for seniors?#

Choose games that are enjoyable and social rather than ones that promise to protect memory, because no game has proved that. The Alzheimer's Society says there is "no strong evidence" that brain training lowers dementia risk (Alzheimer's Society). For variety, a collection helps: Puzzle Jar switches games every three levels, and Simon Tatham's Puzzles has 40 logic types. Word and number fans will be happier with crosswords or sudoku, which we do not make. The research is summed up on our puzzle types page.

What are the best puzzle games for kids?#

For young children, games without ads or purchases are the safest pick, such as subscription services built for families; Apple Arcade states its games have no ads or in-app purchases (Apple). Puzzle Jar is made for a general audience, not specially for children, and it shows ads and sells helpers. Read our kids, safety and privacy page before handing it to a child; it explains ratings and how to lock purchases.

What is the best game for a long flight?#

Bring a mix: one game you can sink hours into and one you can drop the moment the meal cart arrives, all downloaded and opened once before you fly. A longer paid puzzle adventure suits a quiet few hours, if you have checked it starts in airplane mode. A collection like Puzzle Jar gives variety from one download, and its levels are short enough to stop mid-descent. Charge your phone, pack headphones, and avoid games that need a connection to start; airline wifi is unreliable and often paid.

What are the most relaxing puzzle games?#

The calmest games share three traits: no clock, no lives to wait for, and no punishment for thinking slowly. In Puzzle Jar, Sweet Stacks, Shelf Sort and Snug Skeins fit that best. From other studios, "stitch." on Apple Arcade (Lykkegaard) is a gentle embroidery puzzle, and A Little to the Left (Secret Mode) is a tidying game you can try free before unlocking the full game. Turn off music if even soft sound distracts you.

What are the best cozy puzzle games for iPhone?#

Cozy usually means soft art, homely themes and no failure stress. Unpacking (Witch Beam, paid, with Unpacking+ on Apple Arcade) is a favorite: you unbox a life into new rooms. Patterned on Apple Arcade is a relaxed pattern puzzle whose page says "No ads. No in-app purchases." In Puzzle Jar, the coziest are Snug Skeins, with woolly felt toys, and Honey Hive, with honey tiles on a comb. Ours is cozy in looks; it still has ads between levels unless removed.

What are the best puzzle games with no timer?#

Most of Puzzle Jar qualifies: 19 of its 20 games show no clock, and the only countdown is Route Duel's optional Solo Run. Eight of our games also have no move limit at all, so you can only lose by filling the space: Bolts & Jars, Sweet Stacks, Button Tin, Nut Sort, Hop On, Snug Skeins, Shelf Sort and Honey Hive. Outside our app, pencil-and-paper style logic collections such as Simon Tatham's Puzzles are a natural fit. Avoid "rush" or "challenge" modes, which often add one.

Which sorting games have no timer?#

In Puzzle Jar every sorting game plays without a clock: Nut Sort, Sweet Stacks, Shelf Sort, Snug Skeins and the screw game Bolts & Jars. None counts your moves either; you only get stuck when every free space fills. Shelf Sort even waits for you: after 40 moves without a pop it offers help or a "KEEP TRYING" button, nothing more. Some popular sorting games elsewhere add timed levels, so read the store description first.

Which puzzle games have no lives?#

Puzzle Jar has no lives or energy anywhere, so a lost level can be retried instantly, as often as you want. That includes Bead Swap, Fruit Tumble and our other level-based games, which in many apps come with lives. A few of our games use hearts inside a level, such as 3 hearts in Quilt Pins and Pencil Case, but losing them only ends that try. If lives are what you dislike elsewhere, read a game's store description for "lives" or "energy" before installing.

What are the best puzzle games without ads?#

For truly ad-free play, pay once or subscribe. Apple Arcade says its games come with "no ads" and no in-app purchases (Apple); Netflix says the same of its games (Netflix Help). Paid apps such as Mini Metro and Monument Valley 2 show no ads label on Google Play. Free and ad-free: Simon Tatham's Puzzles. Puzzle Jar is free with ads between levels; a one-time Remove Ads purchase ends the full-screen ones in all 20 games.

What are the best sorting games without ads?#

On iPhone, Apple Arcade lists "Water Sort Puzzle: Get Color+" from Tripledot Studios, an ad-free color sort for subscribers. On Android there is no Arcade, so check each game's store page for the "Contains ads" label. Puzzle Jar's five sorting games (Nut Sort, Sweet Stacks, Shelf Sort, Snug Skeins and Bolts & Jars) are free with ads, and one Remove Ads purchase covers all of them, so you do not pay per game. See what Remove Ads does.

What are the best sorting games for adults?#

Adults tend to want sorting with real decisions, not just obvious moves. Our picks from Puzzle Jar: Nut Sort, where bolts of different heights and hidden dark nuts force planning, and Shelf Sort, where goods hidden behind the front row change what is safe. For a classic, Water Sort Puzzle by IEC Global is the long-standing liquid version. Every third level is a hard one and every fourth hard level is a boss, so the challenge keeps returning.

What is the best screw puzzle game?#

Screw Jam by Rollic Games is the name most people know, free on iPhone and Android. Many copies share the screw-puzzle name, so look at the developer before installing. Our own, Bolts & Jars, is a strong pick if you dislike rushing: no clock, no move limit, a 5-hole tray, and every level checked by a solver as winnable without help. It comes with 19 other games in the same download, which matters if you tire of one genre.

What is the best nuts and bolts game?#

If you mean the color-sorting kind, the big names include Tripledot's "Nut Sort: Color Sorting Game". Our Nut Sort mixes in tall and short bolts in changing board shapes from level 7, and dark nuts on hard levels. If you mean screws in plates, that is a screw puzzle; try Bolts & Jars. And if you came for the snack mix of the same name, this page cannot help, sorry.

What are the best tile matching games for adults?#

Triple Tile (Tripledot Studios), Tile Busters (Spyke Games) and Zen Match (Moon Active) are the well-known three. In Puzzle Jar, Button Tin is our tile match, with piles growing to about 70-96 buttons and face-down buttons on later hard levels, which gives adults more to plan. It never limits moves; you lose only if the 7-space card fills with no three alike. People who prefer classic pairs matching may like a mahjong solitaire app better.

What are the best offline match-3 games?#

Look for games whose levels are built in, not streamed. Bead Swap in Puzzle Jar plays fully offline, with specials, wax, crates and gaps, on 7x7 to 9x9 trays. Royal Match (Dream Games) and Candy Crush Saga (King) are the famous free alternatives and very polished, but we have not tested them offline, so try them in airplane mode before a trip.

What is the best bubble shooter game offline?#

For offline play, our Bubble Picnic works with no signal and has no lives and no lowering ceiling, with a dotted aim guide that shows bounces. The classic free names are Bubble Witch 3 Saga by King and the "Bubble Shooter" apps by Ilyon, both free to download with ads and purchases according to their Google Play pages. If you want a bubble shooter on an iPad, choose one of those, since Puzzle Jar has no iPad version.

What are the best block puzzle games?#

For endless, score-chasing play, Block Blast! by Hungry Studio is the obvious one, and it has an ad-free "Block Blast!+" on Apple Arcade. The official Tetris app from PlayStudios is the pick for falling blocks. For levels with a goal, Mosaic Tray in Puzzle Jar deals a fixed, limited set of pieces so each level has a planned solution, and you free jewels instead of chasing a score. Those who want blocks that slide out of a box should try Toy Chest.

What are the best logic puzzle games for phone?#

Logic fans have good free options. Simon Tatham's Puzzles collects 40 classic logic types, free and open source, with ports on Android and iPhone (Simon Tatham). LinkedIn's daily games include Queens and Tango. Our Quilt Pins is a Star Battle-type grid with a bank of 2,000 quilts, harder steps at set levels, and a hint that explains why a pin must go where it does. If you love sudoku, Apple Arcade has "Good Sudoku+".

What are good puzzle games for a two-minute break?#

Pick games with short levels and no penalty for leaving mid-way. We built Honey Hive levels to last about two minutes, and early levels in every Puzzle Jar game are small. Garland Tangle levels are only 4 to 24 moves. A daily puzzle, like LinkedIn's or Puzzle Jar's "Today's puzzles", is another neat fit: one puzzle, done, back to work. Endless games are worse for breaks because there is no natural stopping point.

What puzzle games can you play with one hand?#

Tap games are easiest one-handed in portrait. Puzzle Jar runs in portrait only, and most of its games are single taps: Bolts & Jars, Button Tin, Hop On, Pencil Case, Snug Skeins and the three blast games, Fruit Tumble, Balloon Bunch and Tide Pool. Games that need a drag, such as Mosaic Tray, Honey Hive and Shelf Sort (which also accepts tap-then-tap), work one-handed too, just slower. On a large phone, use the phone's one-handed or reachability mode to bring the top of the screen down.

What are good puzzle games for a phone with little storage?#

A collection saves space compared with many single-game apps, because each app carries its own engine and files. Puzzle Jar fits 20 games in about 106 MB on iPhone. Simple logic collections like Simon Tatham's Puzzles, with plain graphics, are another light option. Before installing, check the size on the store page, and remember games can grow with updates. If storage is very tight, play web puzzles such as 2048 in the browser instead.

Which apps have many puzzle games in one?#

Puzzle Jar puts 20 games in one app, from screw and nut sorting to match-3, a bubble shooter and a logic grid, with one shared level road and shared hints. Simon Tatham's Puzzles has 40 logic puzzles in one free app. LinkedIn offers several daily puzzles in its app. One limit of ours: every game is a color-and-shape puzzle, with no word, card or number games among them.

Are there free puzzle games with no in-app purchases?#

Yes, but they are rare. Simon Tatham's Puzzles is free, open source and shows no ads or purchases labels on Google Play. LinkedIn says anyone, member or not, can play its daily puzzles. Puzzle Jar is not one of them: it is free to download and has in-app purchases (Remove Ads, hint packs, take backs, rescues and a one-time Starter Pack), though every level can be won without buying anything. Subscriptions like Apple Arcade avoid purchases inside games, but the subscription itself costs money.

What are good games for anxiety and stress?#

If you want a game to unwind, look for one with no clock, no lives and nothing to lose by stopping: Sweet Stacks and Shelf Sort fit, as do cozy titles like Unpacking. Research hints that a game hard enough to absorb you, but not too hard, helps most while waiting on worrying news (UC Riverside, Tetris study). Games are not a treatment. If anxiety is affecting your days, the NHS advises seeing a GP (NHS).

What are the best brain training games?#

We would pick games you enjoy over games that promise a sharper brain. A 2014 statement from the Stanford Center on Longevity said brain game claims "are frequently exaggerated and at times misleading" (Stanford). Logic puzzles like Quilt Pins or Simon Tatham's collection give real thinking work, and switching between different puzzle types keeps it fresh. Physical activity, hearing care and staying social are among the changeable dementia risks named by the 2024 Lancet Commission (Livingston et al.), which is stronger ground than any app.

What are the best merge games offline?#

Suika Game by Aladdin X is the famous drop-and-merge game, sold as a paid app, with "Suika Game+" on Apple Arcade; we have not tested it offline, so try airplane mode first. Treat Drop is the merge game in Puzzle Jar and works without a signal; it gives each level a goal and a fixed order of sweets, rather than one endless round. Board-style merge games that build a town or garden are a different genre and often expect a connection for events.

What are the best bus jam games?#

Bus Jam by Rollic Games and Bus Out are two well-known names on the stores. In Puzzle Jar, Hop On is our bus jam, built to be played slowly: no clock, the next buses always visible, and a bench that grows from 4 to 7 seats. New twists arrive one at a time, from planters at level 5 to big six-seat buses at level 65, so you learn each one before the next.

What are the best hexa sort games?#

Hexa Sort from Lion Studios Plus is the well-known name, and many hexagon stacking games share the idea. In Puzzle Jar, Honey Hive is our take, on combs of 12 to 21 cells with 4 to 7 colors. Its locked cells open only by collecting tiles, never by an ad, and its rescue sends bees to lift the most mixed-up stacks off the comb. If you like chain reactions, it is a good second hexa game to keep.

What are the best rope untangle games?#

Twisted Tangle and Tangle Master 3D, both by Rollic Games, are the big names in rope puzzles. The pure logic version is Untangle in Simon Tatham's free collection, which is just points and lines. Our Garland Tangle sits in between: strings of lights between lanterns on posts, a move count per level from 4 to 24, and no timer or lives. Searching "rope untangle game no ads"? Ours has ads between levels unless you buy Remove Ads.

What are the best yarn sort games?#

Wool and yarn puzzles are a recent genre; Knit Out (Rollic Games) and Yarn Fever (Brave HK) are two of the names on the stores. Our Snug Skeins is the yarn game in Puzzle Jar: felt toys unwind layer by layer into baskets of their colors, with a sewing tin of 4 to 7 spots for spare wool. Toys show their next color and how many layers are left, so you can plan instead of guessing.

What are the best tap-to-blast games?#

Toon Blast by Peak is the well-known one. Puzzle Jar has three tap-to-blast games with different physics, which is unusual in one app: Fruit Tumble (fruit falls), Balloon Bunch (balloons rise) and Tide Pool (rows push up as you tap). None of the three has lives or a clock: Fruit Tumble and Balloon Bunch give each level a set number of moves, and in Tide Pool the tide rises by taps, not seconds.

What are the best puzzle games for iPad?#

Not Puzzle Jar, honestly: we only make an iPhone version for Apple devices. For iPad, Monument Valley and Monument Valley 2 (ustwo games) use the big screen beautifully, Unpacking is listed for iPad, and Simon Tatham's Puzzles has an iPhone and iPad port. If you play on an Android tablet, our help page covers how Puzzle Jar behaves there.

What are the best puzzle games with no words or numbers?#

If you want to play without reading or counting, every game in Puzzle Jar works by color, shape and position; the rules card is the only text. That makes it good for people learning English or tired of word games. On the other hand, if words or numbers are exactly what you want, Puzzle Jar is the wrong app; look at LinkedIn's daily games or "Good Sudoku+".

What is the best puzzle game app in 2026?#

There is no single best one; it depends on whether you want one deep game or many light ones. For one game, Monument Valley 3 by ustwo games is a highly polished choice that you can try free before unlocking the full game. For variety without paying, Puzzle Jar gives 20 games and thousands of levels free, with ads unless removed. For no ads at all on iPhone, an Apple Arcade subscription covers many puzzle games. We are biased about ours, so try two or three and keep the one you open every day.

What are good games to play on your phone when you're bored?#

Pick something that is ready the moment you open it and lets you stop without losing anything. Puzzle games suit this better than most: no matches to wait for, no team to let down. In Puzzle Jar the Play button picks up the next level at once, and if you are tired of one game, Free play lets you jump to any of the 20. If you would rather have one long game to lose yourself in, a story puzzle such as Monument Valley 3 is a better fit than a collection of short levels.

What are good road trip games with no wifi?#

For passengers, offline single-player puzzle games are the safest choice, because mobile signal drops on long drives. Download everything at home and open each game once while you still have a connection. Puzzle Jar plays every game offline, and Route Duel's Solo Run is the only part with a clock, so nobody is rushed when the car hits a bumpy stretch. If someone gets car sick reading or looking down, short levels with breaks to look out of the window help more than a long session.

Is there a screw, sort or tile puzzle game with no ads?#

Yes, but usually you pay for it once or by subscription. Apple Arcade has sort and block games with no ads, such as "Water Sort Puzzle: Get Color+" and "Block Blast!+". Most free screw, nut, tile, yarn and goods sort games show ads and sell a remove-ads option per game. Puzzle Jar works the same way, with one difference: its one-time Remove Ads purchase covers all 20 of its games at once, from the screw puzzle to the yarn sort. Optional reward videos stay, but nothing plays unless you tap for it.
