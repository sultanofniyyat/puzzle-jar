Bead Swap: the match-3 game

By the Sultan of Niyyat team, who make Puzzle Jar. Last updated 2026-11-24.

Bead Swap is the match-3 game in Puzzle Jar: glass beads in a wooden craft tray. Swipe a bead onto its neighbor to line up three or more of one color; they clear and the beads above drop down. Each level sets goals at the top, such as collecting beads, melting wax, breaking crates or bringing brass buttons down, and gives you between 15 and 38 moves to reach them.

How do you play Bead Swap?#

Swipe one bead onto the bead next to it, up, down, left or right. If the swap makes a line of three or more of one color, the line clears, the beads above fall into the space and new beads drop in from the top. Watch the goal counters above the tray; when every one reaches zero, the level is won.

Why did my swap slide back in Bead Swap?#

Because it did not line anything up. A swap that makes no match of three slides straight back to where it was, and it costs no move. So a mistaken swipe is harmless; only swaps that clear something take a move off your count.

What do the needle, burst and rainbow beads do?#

  • Needle (line up 4 in a row): clears its whole row or column.
  • Burst (an L or T shape): clears the squares around it.
  • Rainbow bead (5 in a row): clears every bead of the color you swap it with.

Tap a needle or burst, or swap it, to set it off; a rainbow bead goes off when you swap it with a bead of the color you want gone. The first time a level makes each kind of special, a short tip under the tray says what it does.

What happens if I swap two special beads together?#

You get a bigger blast than either would make alone. A needle with a needle clears a row and a column; a burst with a needle sweeps three rows and three columns; two bursts clear a big square; a rainbow bead with another special turns every bead of that color into the same special; and two rainbow beads clear the whole tray. Saving specials until two sit side by side is often worth a move.

How do you clear wax and crates in Bead Swap?#

Wax melts when beads lying on it clear, so make matches right on top of the wax spots. Crates break when beads next to them clear, so match alongside them. Thick wax (from level 19) and strong crates (from level 25) need two hits each. Needles and bursts help a lot, since one blast can hit several spots at once.

How do you get the brass buttons down?#

Clear the beads underneath them. Buttons fall like beads but can never be matched, and they count only when they reach the bottom row. A needle running up a button's column is the quickest way to drop it several squares at once. Buttons arrive on Bead Swap level 13.

What are the holes in the Bead Swap tray?#

They are gaps, squares where no bead can sit. From level 10 some trays have cut corners, a hole in the middle or notches in the sides. Beads still fall straight down, slipping past the gap, so the columns keep filling, but a gap breaks up lines and limits where you can match.

What happens when you run out of moves in Bead Swap?#

The level stops and offers +5 MOVES, a fresh try or the way home. +5 MOVES can be used up to twice on a level; the first time you can earn it with a short video you choose, the second time it uses a rescue. A take back also hands back the move it undoes.

Are Bead Swap levels rigged so you need to pay?#

No. The beads that drop in are fixed for each level, so a level plays out the same way every time and can never turn harder in secret. Before a level ships, a computer player wins it with no help, and our tests replay that win. If the tray ever has no possible swap, the beads are mixed, and that is fixed too.

Why is a bead glowing when I didn't ask for a hint?#

That is the free nudge. If you do nothing for 6 seconds, Bead Swap makes a working swap glow, as most match-3 games do. It is free and only shows a swap that matches; it is not the best move. The paid HINT is the one that looks for the best swap.

How does the Bead Swap hint choose a move?#

It plays out every possible swap, knowing which beads will drop in next, and shows the one that does most for your goals. That is more than a person can see from the board, which is why it can point at a move that looks plain but sets up a chain. Hints are shared by all 20 games.

What is the best strategy for Bead Swap?#

Spend moves on goals, not on pretty matches. Check which counters are furthest from zero and work there first. Make a needle or burst whenever you can, and aim it: a needle placed in a column full of wax does several moves' work. Late in a level, count your moves left against what remains, and use specials before you run out rather than saving them.

How hard does Bead Swap get?#

Trays grow from 7 by 7 to 9 by 9 squares, and new obstacles come one at a time: wax at level 3, crates at 7, gaps at 10, buttons at 13, then thick wax and strong crates. Five colors are in play, six on later boss levels. Hard levels give just enough moves for a good plan, with little to spare.
